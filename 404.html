How Social Shelf Works

Borrowing and sharing books has never been this effortless and community-driven.

01

Discover & Reserve

Browse our decentralized campus shelf. Filter by genre, author, or availability, and reserve in one click.

02

Pick Up & Enjoy

Collect your book from our campus library hub or designated reader lockers with zero rental fees.

03

Return or Donate

Return the book within 14 days or donate your finished books to give someone else the joy of reading.
